## Language: English
- Natural spoken English, the cadence of a LinkedIn founder or a motivational business reel. Contractions are fine.
- Short sentences that are easy to say aloud; no abbreviations the voice could misread (write "for example").
- Numbers as words unless very short ("five a.m.", "zero employees").
- "caption" = exactly the same words as "text".
- Title: max 60 characters, curiosity, no clickbait lies, may end with one emoji.
- Hashtags: 4-5, e.g. #ceo #startup #relatable #comedy #hustle.
