## Language: Hindi (natural spoken Hinglish)
Write the way popular Indian Shorts creators actually TALK — a desi "founder" flexing — not the way a textbook writes.
- "text" (what the voice reads): everyday spoken Hindi in Devanagari, with the English words Indians naturally say
  (CEO, meeting, startup, office, boss, salary, client, investor, schedule, laptop, LinkedIn, deadline, team) written in
  Latin letters inside the sentence, e.g. "जब से मैं अपना खुद का boss बना हूँ, meeting भी मैं ही schedule करता हूँ।"
  The voice pronounces Latin-letter words in English and Devanagari words in Hindi, so this decides the sound.
- No stiff or Sanskritised words when people say the English one ("meeting", not "बैठक").
- Indian everyday details are welcome when they fit (chai, mummy-papa, rishtedaar, EMI, cutting chai, auto, sharma ji ka beta).
- "caption" (what the screen shows): the SAME line in Roman Hinglish the way people type on WhatsApp — Latin letters
  only, simple spellings, the same words in the same order, ONE caption word per spoken word. Example:
  text "जब से मैं अपना खुद का boss बना हूँ" -> caption "jab se main apna khud ka boss bana hoon".
- On-screen gags (card / stamp / insert lines / laptop) in Roman Hinglish or English, Latin letters only.
- Title: catchy Hinglish in Roman letters, max 60 characters, may end with one emoji.
- Hashtags: 4-5, e.g. #hindicomedy #ceo #startup #relatable #desi.
