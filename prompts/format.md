## The channel format: "the jobless CEO"
Dio's Stories is a comedy Shorts/Reels channel. Every video is one 20-30 second skit told in FIRST PERSON by "the CEO":
a box-headed guy in a tie who talks exactly like a hustle-culture founder or a motivational business reel, deadpan and
proud. As the video goes on, it slowly becomes clear that he has no company, no job, no money, no employees — he is
the CEO of nothing. The comedy is the gap between how seriously he talks and what is actually going on.

Shape of every script (5-8 short spoken lines):
1. HOOK (line 1, under 3 seconds): a confident flex that sounds like a real CEO or hustle reel. No joke yet.
   Examples of the energy (never copy these): "I wake up at five because empires don't build themselves."
   "Since I became my own boss, my calendar has been fully booked."
2. BUILD (2-4 lines): each line still sounds legit, but carries a small crack the viewer starts to notice.
3. TWIST (one line, the punch line): the plain reveal — he has no business / no job / no office / no money.
   Short, flat, specific. This is the line marked as `punch_line`.
4. TAG (optional, one line): a tiny afterthought that makes it worse ("My mom calls this unemployment. I call it stealth mode.").

Rules:
- The narrator never winks at the camera and never explains the joke. Deadpan.
- Specific beats generic: "I cancelled the meeting because I was the meeting" beats "I am lazy".
- Every line must be SHOWABLE in the cartoon world (his home office, a meeting room with empty chairs, a cafe he calls
  his office, a city street, his room at night) — an action, an object, a screen, a reaction.
- Relatable, kind humour: he is the joke. No punching down, no real people, no real companies or brands, no politics,
  no religion, nothing sexual, nothing about real tragedies, no medical or money advice.
- Original: never copy another creator's lines, titles or catchphrases.
